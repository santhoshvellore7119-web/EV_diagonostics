## 2. Analog Front-End (AFE) Schematic Specifications

### 2.1 Ultrasonic Pulser & Time-of-Flight Front-End
- **PZT Excitation**: High-speed MOSFET driver TC4420EPA driven by ESP32 RMT peripheral, switching $V_{\text{boost}} = +48\,\text{V}$ through a pulse inductor $L_{\text{pulse}} = 4.7\,\mu\text{H}$ to generate a fast $100\,\text{ns}$ excitation pulse with $dV/dt > 500\,\text{V}/\mu\text{s}$.
- **LNA Stage**: Ultra-low noise JFET operational amplifier AD8065 ($e_n = 7\,\text{nV}/\sqrt{\text{Hz}}$, $GBW = 145\,\text{MHz}$) configured in non-inverting bandpass mode ($f_c = 1.0\,\text{MHz}$, Gain $= 40\,\text{dB}$, $Q=4$).
- **Zero-Crossing Comparator**: TLV3501 (4.5 ns propagation delay, rail-to-rail) with adjustable hysteresis to prevent false triggering from transducer ringing.
- **Time-to-Digital Converter**: Texas Instruments TDC7200 configured in Mode 2 (multi-cycle averaging) achieving $55\,\text{ps}$ RMS timing resolution over SPI.

### 2.2 Electrical Precision Sensing
- **Texas Instruments INA226**: 16-bit Sigma-Delta ADC with programmable conversion times ($140\,\mu\text{s}$ to $8.24\,\text{ms}$) and averaging up to 1024 samples.
- **Shunt Resistor**: $10\,\text{m}\Omega, 0.1\%, 5\,\text{W}$ Kelvin 4-terminal resistor (Bourns CSS2H-2512R-L010F) providing $100\,\mu\text{A}$ current resolution.

### 2.3 Synchronous Active Rebalancer Power Stage
- **Top & Bottom Switches**: ON Semiconductor FDMS86180 N-Channel PowerTrench MOSFETs ($V_{\text{DS}} = 100\,\text{V}, R_{\text{DS(on)}} = 3.2\,\text{m}\Omega, I_D = 60\,\text{A}$).
- **Gate Driver**: TI TPS28225 high-speed synchronous buck driver with adaptive dead-time control ($25\,\text{ns}$ dead-time) preventing shoot-through.
- **Energy Storage Inductor**: Coilcraft SER2918H-103KL ($10\,\mu\text{H}, I_{\text{sat}} = 14.5\,\text{A}, DCR = 3.1\,\text{m}\Omega$).
- **Efficiency**: $> 92.4\%$ at $2.5\,\text{A}$ continuous transfer current between adjacent battery cells.
